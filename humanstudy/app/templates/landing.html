Watch one short video, then answer questions about it

Each question asks about one specific person in the video: what they are wearing, what they do, where they are, or what they are holding. The person is described at one moment and asked about at another, so you will need to follow them as the scene changes.

  1. Watch the video once, all the way through. It runs about two to four minutes.
  2. Answer the questions one at a time. There are usually twelve to eighteen of them.
  3. You can replay or scrub the video at any point while answering.
  4. Review your answers, then submit. The whole thing takes about fifteen to twenty minutes.

Before you start

Every question has exactly one correct answer, and the questions are hard on purpose. Answer as best you can rather than rushing. A laptop or desktop works better than a phone.

We record your answers, how long each question took, and how you used the video player. Nothing that identifies you is collected or stored.
